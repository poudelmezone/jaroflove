## ✨ Features

- 📜 **36 Interactive Love Notes**: Scattered throughout a layered glass jar, each opening with a floating 3D unfold animation.
- 🌸 **Floral Aesthetics**: Charming custom flower graphics paired with glassmorphism visual styling.
- 🎵 **Ambient Background Music**: Integrated background audio featuring *"Nothing"* by Bruno Major with smooth user-interaction playback.
- 🫨 **Physics & Easter Eggs**:
  - **Single Click / Tap**: Shakes the jar and redistributes notes naturally inside the glass.
  - **Double Click / Tap**: Triggers a cheerful burst of floating heart particles.
- 💌 **Surprise Final Letter**: Unlocks an exclusive romantic letter overlay once every single note in the jar is opened.
- 🎨 **Layered Visuals**: Sandwiched SVG glass reflections, soft drop shadows, floating hero sparkles, and star fields.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive**: Crafted to look stunning on both mobile displays and desktop screens.
